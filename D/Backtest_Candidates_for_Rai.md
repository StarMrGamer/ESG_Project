## 1. Sembcorp Industries — signals ran years ahead of recognition
Sep 2020 Sembcorp Marine demerger → May 2021 brown-to-green Investor Day (10 GW renewables target) → Jun 2021 inaugural S$400M green bond → Aug 2021 Singapore's first energy-sector sustainable financing framework → **29 Sep 2021 S$675M sustainability-linked bond, IFC's first SLB investment globally** → Sep 2022 SEIL (India coal) sale announced, completed Jan 2023 → recognition arrives late: FTSE4Good (2025), **MSCI AAA as of Mar 2026**.
*Caveats:* exact MSCI upgrade path/dates unverified. The SEIL sale used a deferred payment note, criticised as retaining coal exposure — treat as a contested-signal discussion point, which is itself good methodology material.

## 2. ACEN — transition milestones before recognition
Feb 2019 CBI-certified US$ green bonds (among SE Asia's first) → Jun 2019 AC Energy–Phinma merger → Oct 2021 first Philippine energy company to commit Net Zero 2050 → **7 Nov 2022 completes the world's first market-based ETM: P7.2bn deal retiring the 246 MW SLTEC coal plant by 2040 vs 2057** → Dec 2023 COP28 deal with the Coal-to-Clean Credit Initiative to explore 2030 retirement via transition credits.
*Caveats:* no dated MSCI/Sustainalytics action verifiable — recognition here is deal "firsts," not rating moves; say so. ETM is debated (plant still runs to 2040 under the original deal); Ayala ownership complicates attribution.

## 3. Top Glove — the deterioration a stable score masked (the money case)
**Dec 2018** Guardian investigation links its factories to forced-labour indicators (Top Glove's own rebuttal confirms the article) → **2019–2020** continuing NGO/media reports on recruitment fees and debt bondage → **15 Jul 2020 US CBP Withhold Release Order** → Nov–Dec 2020 massive dormitory COVID outbreak → Mar 2021 CBP formal forced-labour Finding and seizures → Apr 2021 US$30M+ worker remediation → Sep 2021 ban lifted → **Nov 2021: Top Glove retained on the Dow Jones Sustainability Index for a third straight year** — the entire static-score critique in one datapoint. Quantified cost: RM3.6bn revenue lost during restrictions (MITI).
*Why this is the strongest case:* the warning signals (Dec 2018, 2019) sit squarely inside a pre-Jul-2020 lookback window, the outcome is a US government record, and the incumbent-score failure (DJSI retention through the whole episode) is documented. Low factual risk.

## 4. Adaro — where a naive momentum engine gets fooled (the mandatory "wrong" case)
Dec 2021–2022 announces US$2bn aluminium smelter in a "green industrial park," heavy "Adaro Green" branding and EV-supply-chain narrative → **Mar–May 2023 DBS, OCBC, Standard Chartered decline/withdraw financing over the coal reality; five Indonesian banks lend US$1.5bn instead** → Jun 2023 Mongabay: smelter to run on 2.2 GW of new captive coal until at least 2030 (~5.2 Mt CO2e/yr) → May 2024 NGO pressure escalates → Dec 2024 thermal-coal spin-off (Adaro Andalan IPO), parent renamed Alamtri; NGOs call the spin-off itself greenwashing.
*How to present it:* announcement-flow momentum at Feb 2023 was strongly positive; the outcome was financing rejection and reputational deterioration. This is the case that proves why the engine discounts forward-looking language and caps company-PR source quality at 0.5 — and if the engine still gets it wrong, that IS the published failure case, with the fix explained. Bonus teaching point: the rename/spin-off is a live entity-resolution hazard.
*Caveats:* company contests the greenwashing label; no dated rating action verifiable; exact smelter announcement date (Dec 2021 vs early 2022) unconfirmed.

## 5. REE Corp — honest uncertainty on thin coverage
2020–2021 builds ~1 GW hydro/wind/solar portfolio → **11 Aug 2022 HSBC arranges VND900bn (~US$38.5M) green loan for its LEED-certified e.town 6 building** → reported VNSI constituent (no primary dated source found). Coverage is sparse, largely Vietnamese-language, and conflates real-estate vs power greening. No MSCI/Sustainalytics/FTSE4Good coverage found — the absence is the point. **Expected engine behaviour: few signals, low confidence, wide uncertainty band, and the UI showing that honestly.** This also demonstrates the Vietnam language limitation named in Methodology §2/§8.

## Cross-cutting cautions
- MSCI historical rating-action dates were not publicly verifiable for cases 2–5 — never claim "the rating didn't move" without a source; claim "public recognition lagged," which is documented.
- Cases 1 and 4 contain disputed narratives (DPN coal exit; spin-off greenwashing) — use as contested-signal discussion, not settled fact.
- All five outcome events are public and precisely dated, so the cutoff assertion (`signal.published_at < case.cutoff_date`) is enforceable in the harness.
